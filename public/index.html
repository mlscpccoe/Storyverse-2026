01 / MISSION BRIEFING

Write the story.
Then build the world.

StoryVerse is a two-round competition designed to help students combine creative writing with practical software development.

Participants choose an issue connected to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals — such as clean energy, waste management, or accessible education. In the first round, teams present the problem and solution through a creative story or comic strip. In the second round, shortlisted teams take their story and turn it into a playable game or an interactive website.

02 / THE ROUNDS

Two rounds. One narrative.

ROUND 01 · 9 OCT 2026

The Story Sprint

10:20 AM – 12:20 PM

Teams must select a real-world social or environmental cause aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. Within the 2-hour sprint, teams must produce an original narrative script or illustrated comic strip (minimum 4 to 8 panels) depicting a systemic community problem, relatable characters, an escalating tension, and an actionable path to resolution.

PERMITTED TOOLS

No restrictions. Canva, Figma, Photoshop, graphic tablets, digital storyboards, or AI tools for scriptwriting, panel generation, character consistency, or dialogue refinement.

SUBMISSION DELIVERABLES
01Final digital comic strip or illustrated story document (PDF form).
02An Impact Brief outlining the chosen SDG, root problem, target community, and the core message.
03Tool Disclosure list identifying primary design platforms or AI models used.

Deadline 12:20 PM sharp. Shortlist for Round 2 announced immediately after judging. Round 1 scores do not carry forward.

ROUND 02 · GRAND FINALE · 9 OCT 2026

Interactive Realms

12:40 PM – 3:40 PM

Shortlisted teams must translate their Round 1 story, characters, and cause-driven conflict into a functional, playable interactive website or video game. The digital product must logically mirror the choices, mechanics, or systemic challenges presented in their Round 1 narrative.

ZERO STACK RESTRICTIONS
HTML5 / CSS / JS React Phaser Three.js Godot Pygame web Twine Scratch Visual novel engines

AI coding assistants and open-source assets are allowed, provided teams understand, explain, and demonstrate the codebase.

FINAL DELIVERABLES
01Live, working link to the deployed website or game.
02GitHub repository link containing the complete source code.
03A 3-minute pitch and gameplay walkthrough before the judging panel, followed by a 2-minute Q&A.

Every team member must be present and able to speak to different aspects of the project during jury review.
